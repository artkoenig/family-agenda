# Merchant of Record for a one-person EU subscription business

Question (issue #215, under map #222): which payment provider fits a German individual operator (Einzelunternehmen) selling a monthly consumer subscription in the EU? Compared: Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, and Stripe with Stripe Tax as the baseline. Stripe's own Merchant of Record product, Managed Payments, is added because Lemon Squeezy is migrating to it.

Research date: 2026-11-06. **Source caveat:** the research sandbox could not fetch paddle.com, lemonsqueezy.com, stripe.com or europa.eu directly (DNS blocked). Every claim below comes from search-engine extracts of the official pages linked, not from reading the pages. The extracts are the providers' own wording, but numbers must be re-checked on the live page before they enter the price. Items marked *unverified* were not found at all.

## Answer in short

- **Paddle is the best fit if it accepts the product.** It is a true Merchant of Record (MoR), has the most complete subscription webhooks, and sole traders skip its business verification. Cost: 5% + 50 cents per transaction, all-in, with monthly payouts above a $100 threshold.
- **Lemon Squeezy has a strategic problem.** Stripe acquired it and is steering users to Stripe Managed Payments. Do not start a new business on a platform that is being folded into another product.
- **Stripe Managed Payments (3.5% on top of Stripe's processing fees) is the MoR alternative to watch**, but eligibility is reviewed per business and its processing and Billing fees add up. Plain Stripe + Stripe Tax is not an MoR: the operator stays the seller and owes the VAT filings.
- The take-rate that enters the price is roughly **5% + 50 cents** (Paddle). On a EUR 5 plan that is about 15%, so the fixed part dominates; price the plan with it, not with the percentage alone.

## Details with sources

### Fees and payouts
- Paddle's published rate is 5% + 50 cents per checkout transaction, described as covering payments, tax, fraud, chargebacks and billing support ([Paddle: MoR overview](https://www.paddle.com/paddle-101), [Paddle vs Stripe](https://www.paddle.com/compare/stripe)). Paddle's comparison claims about Stripe's total cost are marketing; ignore them.
- Paddle payouts: initiated on the 1st of each month, completed usually between the 2nd and 15th; $100 minimum threshold, adjustable up to $100,000; below-threshold balances roll over; payout details are set in the dashboard, not the API ([Paddle: When and how do I get paid](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/when-and-how-do-i-get-paid)). Whether the fee is charged in EUR as 50 euro cents or as a USD amount is *unverified*.
- Lemon Squeezy: 5% + 50 cents, with surcharges of 1.5% international, 1.5% PayPal and 0.5% on subscription payments; payouts cost 0% for US bank accounts and 1% for non-US accounts ([Fees](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees), [Pricing](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing), [payout fee post](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/payout-fees-sliced)). For a German payee, expect about 5.5% + 50 cents, plus 1.5% on non-domestic cards, plus 1% at payout.
- Stripe Tax: 0.5% per transaction with the no-code integration, or $0.50 per transaction via API, charged only where a tax registration is active ([Stripe Tax pricing](https://stripe.com/tax/pricing), [help](https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-stripe-tax-pricing)). Billing pay-as-you-go: 0.7% of Billing volume ([Billing pricing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing)); the extract flagged the page as possibly old. The base EU card rate was not found.
- Stripe Managed Payments: 3.5% per successful transaction, calculated on the amount including VAT, on top of standard processing fees; dispute fees, Billing fees and local payment method fees are extra ([Managed Payments pricing](https://support.stripe.com/questions/managed-payments-pricing)).

### Who owns VAT, invoices, refunds, withdrawal
- EU rule: for cross-border B2C electronic services, the place of supply is the customer's Member State. A supplier established in one single Member State may keep charging home VAT below an EU-wide EUR 10,000 annual threshold; above it, customer-country VAT applies, and the Union One Stop Shop (OSS) lets the supplier file one return instead of registering everywhere ([European Commission OSS portal](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en), [OSS guide, July 2026](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/document/download/55f4ec9d-83e6-4942-9e8d-11d44c243087_en?filename=OSS+Guidelines_revised_1Jan2027_0.pdf)). Without an MoR, the operator therefore files OSS returns; Stripe Tax only calculates.
- Withdrawal right: consumers have 14 days to withdraw from distance contracts unless an Article 16 exception applies. The digital-content exception needs performance to have begun, prior express consent, acknowledgement of losing the right and the trader's confirmation, and the CJEU reads it strictly ([Directive 2011/83/EU, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32011L0083)). In C-234/25 (9 July 2026) the Court held a streaming service is not "digital content" for Article 16(m) ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A62025CJ0234)). A hosted Family Agenda is arguably a service, not digital content, so **plan for the 14-day refund to be honoured on the first payment** rather than relying on the exception. Read only as search extracts; this is not legal advice.
- Paddle, as MoR and authorised reseller, runs the refund process: transactions are non-refundable unless law requires, but the 14-day statutory withdrawal right is honoured in the EU/EEA/UK for one-off purchases and the **first subscription payment, not renewals**; refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days of approval ([Paddle Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy), [Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms)). Older 2022 buyer terms read as discretionary; the current ones control.
- With plain Stripe, the operator is the seller: own terms, own withdrawal handling, own refunds and invoices, own OSS filing. Managed Payments covers tax handling and eligible disputes ([pricing page above](https://support.stripe.com/questions/managed-payments-pricing)); who processes the withdrawal refund there was not found (*unverified*).

### Fit for an individual
- Paddle account verification has three steps: domain review, business verification, identity verification. Business verification is not required for individuals or sole traders; they pass an identity check via Sumsub ([Account verification](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification/what-is-account-verification), [Business identification](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification/what-is-business-verification), [Identity verification](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification/what-is-identity-verification)). Paddle is built for software companies and rejects physical goods and human services; a hosted software subscription is in scope ([Acceptable Use Policy](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle)). Approval criteria for sole traders are not stated; approval risk cannot be ruled out until applied.
- Lemon Squeezy: questionnaire, then ID upload; approval about 1 to 3 business days; one owner per store (no partnerships); subscriptions are allowed, services are not; a failed re-verification pauses payouts and pay-ins ([Getting approved](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/email/approval), [Verify your identity](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/verify-your-identity), [Activate your store](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/activate-your-store)).
- Lemon Squeezy direction: the 2026 update says users are being moved to Stripe Managed Payments (35+ countries, public access pending, free migration) ([2026 update](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/2026-update), [Stripe acquisition](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/stripe-acquires-lemon-squeezy)). No sunset date or closed signups were found.
- Stripe: the Services Agreement covers businesses including sole proprietors in the country where Stripe provides the services ([Stripe Services Agreement](https://stripe.com/legal/ssa-service-terms)). Managed Payments needs an eligibility review on business type and geography, direct integrations only, and acceptable payment performance; it is available in 39 countries (April 2026), but the list and sole-proprietor treatment were not retrieved ([eligibility](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility), [overview](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments)). Whether Germany is on the list is *unverified*.

### Webhooks and provisioning an Instance
- Paddle Billing: `subscription.created`, `subscription.activated`, `subscription.trialing`, `subscription.past_due`, `subscription.canceled`, `subscription.updated`. Its guide recommends granting access on `subscription.created`, keeping access while `past_due`, revoking on `canceled` (a cancel at period end is a scheduled change that fires on the next billing date), deduplicating on `event_id` and ordering by `occurred_at` ([Provision access and handle subscription state](https://developer.paddle.com/build/subscriptions/provision-access-webhooks/), [subscription.canceled](https://developer.paddle.com/webhooks/subscriptions/subscription-canceled/), [subscription.past_due](https://developer.paddle.com/webhooks/subscriptions/subscription-past-due/), [Webhooks overview](https://developer.paddle.com/webhooks/overview)).
- Lemon Squeezy: `subscription_created`, `subscription_cancelled` (grace period until the period end), `subscription_expired` (cancelled period ended, or dunning finished), `subscription_updated`; signed with `X-Signature`; endpoint must return 200, up to three retries ([Event types](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/webhooks/event-types), [Sync with webhooks](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/guides/developer-guide/webhooks)).
- Stripe: Billing subscription webhooks were not retrieved in this pass (*unverified* in detail). Managed Payments works through Checkout ([set-up guide](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/set-up)).
- Both documented providers give the same provisioning shape: create an Instance on the creation event, keep it through `past_due`, stop it on the cancel/expiry event, handle retries idempotently.

## Recommendation and open points

1. Apply to Paddle first. Fallback: Stripe Managed Payments if eligible, else Stripe + Stripe Tax with the operator filing OSS returns.
2. Price with the take-rate **5% + 50 cents**, and budget for first-payment refunds (14-day withdrawal honoured).
3. Before committing, re-check on the live pages: Paddle's currency of the 50-cent fee and its fee base (VAT-inclusive or not); Paddle's sole-trader approval (only an application answers it); Stripe's EU card rate, Billing fee and Managed Payments country list (is Germany included?); Lemon Squeezy's future.
4. Not covered: German Kleinunternehmer status for cross-border EU B2C VAT, GDPR roles of the MoR, Paddle's invoice layout.
