  1. Sound familiar?

    It's 7:15. Is swimming today?

    The timetable is in one app, football practice in another. The class fund is buried in a PDF from school, and the swim gear reminder is somewhere in 84 unread chat messages. In our house, one of us always had to keep it all in their head.

  2. So we built a helper

    It does the reading, so you don't have to.

    Connect the school timetable, your calendars, the school mailbox and the parent chats once. From then on Family Agenda keeps an eye on them, day and night. It only ever reads: it never posts, replies or deletes anything.

  3. The clever part

    It picks out what really matters.

    Long mails, scanned letters, chatty groups: an AI model reads them for you and pulls out what your family needs to know. What's on, what needs doing, and who it's for.

  4. The relief

    And there it is: everything in one place.

    The to-dos sit on top, the days follow below. Everyone sees at a glance where to be and what to bring. No more checking five apps before breakfast, and no more “nobody told me!”

  5. For everyone

    Everyone gets their own Agenda.

    One for each child, one for each parent, one for the whole family. One tap, and Mia sees just Mia's day. It feels most at home on a tablet in the kitchen.

  6. Private

    Your family's life stays yours.

    This is about our kids, so we were strict here. Family Agenda can run on a small computer in your home, like a Raspberry Pi or a NAS, and nothing has to leave the house. No server in the cupboard? We'll host it for you.

Our promises

Built by parents, not by a data business.

  • Your data stays yours

    No ads, no tracking, no selling anything. Your family's data lives where you decide: on your own machine at home, or with us in Germany.

  • It only reads

    It never posts in a chat, marks a mail as read or touches your calendar. Nobody will notice it's there.

  • AI that keeps quiet

    The model that reads your mails can run locally, so not a word goes to a big AI company. And the whole app is open source: anyone can check what it does.

Run it yourself

At home, free and open source

Comfortable with Docker? Three lines on a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or any machine that's always on, and it's yours.

mkdir family-agenda && cd family-agenda
curl -fsSLO https://github.com/artkoenig/family-agenda/releases/latest/download/compose.yaml
docker compose up -d

You'll also need a local LLM. It reads the mails and chats, so nothing leaves your home. Ollama on a machine with 8 to 12 GB of graphics memory does the job.
